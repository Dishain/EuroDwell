The part nobody tells you

Your windows leak. It isn't a defect -
it's the design.

American homes overwhelmingly use double-hung and sliding windows. Both work by having one sash slide past another. For that to slide easily, there has to be a gap - and the only thing in that gap is a brush strip.

Живая схема. На проде - то же плюс видео: купюра, зажатая в европейском окне, не вытаскивается.

01

It's quieter

Sound leaks through gaps and weak frames, not just glass. No gap, no leak.

02

No drafts

Nothing to whistle through when the wind picks up in January.

03

Bills drop

Your HVAC stops paying for the air that leaves through the seal.

04

It stays that way

A brush strip wears out. A gasket under even pressure doesn't.

And you still get both modes: turn the handle one way for a wide inward opening, the other way for secure top-tilt ventilation - no window left open to the street, no screen to fight with.

How we work

We don't just quote.
We work the project.

Most suppliers wait for a purchase order. We start earlier - and stay longer. A typical home takes about three months from first drawing to delivery, and we're in that whole conversation.

We read the drawings

We check openings, egress, spans, glazing and hardware against what you're actually trying to achieve. If something won't work, you'll hear it before it's ordered.

Best solution at the lowest workable cost
