Before you compare prices

Your windows leak. It isn't a defect -
it's the design.

A double-hung or slider works by having one sash slide past another. For it to slide easily there must be a gap, and the only thing in that gap is a brush strip. A tilt-and-turn window doesn't slide - the handle rotates cams around the whole perimeter and pulls the sash into two continuous compression gaskets.

Место под видео: купюра, зажатая в европейском окне, не вытаскивается - в американском выходит свободно. Плюс аудио A/B с dB-метром в кадре.

European uPVC windows in New Jersey

EuroDwell supplies European uPVC tilt-and-turn windows to homeowners, builders and architects across New Jersey, New York and the wider United States. Our systems are manufactured in the EU on Aluplast, Aliplast, Yawal and Drutex profiles, with multi-chamber steel-reinforced frames, warm-edge double or triple glazing and multi-point locking hardware as standard.

Unlike a typical American double-hung or slider, a tilt-and-turn window seals by compression: turning the handle rotates cams around the full perimeter and draws the sash into two continuous gaskets. The result is lower air infiltration, better acoustic performance and no drafts along the meeting rail - and it stays that way, because a compression gasket under even pressure does not wear the way a brush strip does.

Windows are made to your openings rather than to a catalogue of standard sizes, including arched, trapezoidal and oversized units. Finishes cover 46 colour-fast options including lifelike woodgrains, with different colours inside and out. Every order is backed by a 25-year warranty covering the assembly and the hardware. Typical production lead time is around three months; we recommend starting the conversation about four months ahead of your target installation date.

Visit our showroom and warehouse at 307 W. Elizabeth Ave, Linden, NJ, by appointment, and we'll prepare the exact profile sections, glazing units and hardware samples you want to see. Installation is arranged with trained local pros in the Linden / New York City region; elsewhere in the US we connect you with vetted installers and stay involved throughout the project.
